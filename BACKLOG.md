## Website-wide

- Jamaica-specific content: gated subdivisions vs strata differences (no legal advice), local terminology, indicative JMD once a dated rate is set, business WhatsApp contact.
- Lightweight, privacy-conscious analytics: demo completions, calculator use, quote requests, story engagement.
- Keep Core Web Vitals good (LCP ≤ 2.5 s, INP ≤ 200 ms, CLS ≤ 0.1) and the 175 KB first-load JS budget; reduced-motion alternatives for everything animated.

## Not now
