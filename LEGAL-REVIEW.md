# Company and legal pages: items to confirm before the commercial launch

The About, Contact, Privacy and Terms pages contain no invented company details. These
points were left out deliberately and need confirmation (and the Privacy and Terms pages
need review by a lawyer) before the final commercial launch:

1. **Legal entity.** The pages say "operated by Kaimbrya". Add the registered name of the
   LLC, where it is registered, and its registered address once confirmed.
2. **Governing law and jurisdiction.** The Terms have no governing-law clause, because the
   LLC's jurisdiction is not confirmed (an LLC may be registered outside Jamaica).
3. **Data protection law.** Confirm whether Jamaica's Data Protection Act, 2020 applies
   (registration with the Information Commission, a data protection officer, and naming the
   regulator in the policy) and any other law that applies to the LLC.
4. **Retention period.** The policy says requests are kept "only for as long as we need
   them". Set a specific period if required.
5. **Netlify Forms.** The Privacy page describes demo requests being received and stored by
   Netlify and notified to the Gmail inbox. Keep it true: the form must be connected to
   Netlify Forms, with notifications to kaimbryatech@gmail.com, before launch.
6. **Analytics and cookies.** The site currently sets no cookies and has no analytics. If
   Netlify Analytics or any tracking is enabled later, update the "Cookies and analytics"
   section first.
7. **Contact email.** kaimbryatech@gmail.com is used on every page and in the brochure.
